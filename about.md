I am a tenured **Associate Professor in Biomechanics** at the Promobilia MoveAbility Lab, Department of Engineering Mechanics, **KTH Royal Institute of Technology**, Stockholm, Sweden. I am also affiliated with the Department of Women's and Children's Health, **Karolinska Institutet**.

My research focuses on developing computational and experimental approaches to quantify **muscle neuromechanics**, with an emphasis on **motor impairment and neurorehabilitation**. By integrating personalized data from medical imaging, advanced electromyography, wearable sensors, and experimental measurements, I aim to uncover the mechanisms of human movement and advance clinical applications in motor function assessment and recovery.
